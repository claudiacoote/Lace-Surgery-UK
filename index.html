BOOKING TERMS.

Booking & payment

Submit a request on this website and wait for approval before making payment. Full payment is required to secure a slot.

Sending your wig

Wigs must be washed and dried before posting. Wigs that do not appear washed and dried will be returned and a refund processed. Send by tracked courier; Lace Surgery UK is not liable for parcels sent without tracking details.

Return & concerns

Tracked return delivery is included and arranged by Lace Surgery UK. If you are unhappy with the service, report it within 48 hours of receiving your wig.

Express delivery: additional £10, subject to availability. Standard turnaround estimates are 7–10 days (small), 7–14 days (medium) and 14–21 days (large). Please allow for postal transit time.
